Before entering office, Alex Schnell is making his legislative priorities public. These documents — press releases, bill drafts, and proposed amendments — represent the standards Alex intends to apply as a representative. Publishing them in advance is an act of accountability: constituents should not have to guess how their representative will vote on fundamental issues. These are not talking points; they are written, reviewable commitments.

<Tip>
  Alex wants voters to see his standards before he takes office. Reviewing these documents lets you evaluate not just what he says, but what he has actually written and is prepared to introduce on Day One.
</Tip>

## Constitutional grounding

Every document in this archive is drafted with the enumerated powers framework in mind. Legislation that cannot be tied to a specific power in Article I, Section 8 does not belong in Congress — and Alex will not introduce or support bills that depend on expansive readings of the General Welfare, Commerce, or Necessary and Proper clauses to justify their existence.
