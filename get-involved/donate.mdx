This is a grassroots effort built openly by the community. Your support helps keep the campaign transparent, accessible, and focused on the people of Pennsylvania's 3rd Congressional District. Every contribution — no matter the size — helps sustain a campaign built on local voices rather than outside money.

<Warning>
  Contributions are accepted ONLY from individuals whose residential address is within Pennsylvania's 3rd Congressional District (PA-3).
</Warning>

## FEC compliance

Federal law requires campaigns to use their "best efforts" to collect and report the name, mailing address, occupation, and employer of individuals whose contributions exceed **$200 in an election cycle**. The donation form collects this information for all donors to ensure compliance. Providing accurate employer and occupation information is required to complete your donation.

## How to donate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the campaign website">
    Go to [alexschnellpa3.com/donate.html](https://alexschnellpa3.com/donate.html) to access the donation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your donor information">
    Complete all required fields: name, email address, mailing address, employer, and occupation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your eligibility">
    Check all four eligibility boxes to certify that you are a U.S. citizen or permanent resident, contributing your own funds, at least 18 years old, and not a federal contractor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Address verification">
    The site uses the U.S. Census Bureau Geocoding API to confirm your address falls within PA-3. If the Census API is unavailable, a ZIP code fallback is used to verify your location. Submissions from outside the district will not be accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your donation via PayPal">
    After your address is verified, you will be redirected to PayPal to securely complete your contribution.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Eligibility requirements

To contribute to the Alex Schnell for PA-3 campaign, you must meet all of the following criteria:

- U.S. citizen or lawfully admitted permanent resident (green card holder)
- Contributing from your own funds (not reimbursed by another person or entity)
- At least 18 years old
- Not a federal contractor
