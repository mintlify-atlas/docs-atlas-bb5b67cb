Representation begins with listening. The Alex Schnell campaign is built on the principle that a congressional representative should hear from the people they serve — not just at election time, but throughout the work of governing. If you have questions, ideas, concerns, or suggestions about issues affecting PA-3, the campaign wants to hear from you. Every message helps shape a campaign that reflects the real priorities of the district.

## How to send a message

The campaign contact page at [alexschnellpa3.com/contact.html](https://alexschnellpa3.com/contact.html) includes a web form powered by [web3forms](https://web3forms.com/). The form accepts three fields — your name, your email address, and your message — and submits directly to the campaign. There is no account or login required. After submission, you will receive a confirmation on the page and the campaign will follow up by email.

## Community participation

Getting involved does not require filling out a form. There are many ways to connect with the campaign and your neighbors:

- Attending community meetings in your neighborhood
- Sharing local concerns or ideas that affect your daily life
- Volunteering with neighborhood organizations that serve PA-3 residents
- Reaching out about issues affecting your block, school, or community

<Tip>
  Your voice matters. Your perspective matters. This campaign belongs to the community it represents.
</Tip>

## Transparency through GitHub

The campaign website is hosted publicly on GitHub so that any resident can review how it is built, verify that information is accurate, and propose improvements directly. This open structure reflects the campaign's commitment to accountability — the same transparency expected of elected representatives should apply to the campaign itself. If you notice an error or have a suggestion for the site, you can open an issue or pull request in the [campaign repository](https://github.com/AlexSchnellPA3/alexschnellpa3.github.io).
