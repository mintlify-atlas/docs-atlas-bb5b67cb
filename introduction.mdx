Alex Schnell is a member of the Philadelphia 9th Ward Republican Committee exploring a grassroots run for Congress in Pennsylvania's 3rd District. His background in analysis, problem solving, and practical results shapes both how he works and how he approaches the idea of representation.

## Professional background

Alex currently works as a Business Analyst supporting operations and process improvement through a partnership with the Philadelphia Parking Authority. As he describes it:

> "My work focuses on making systems function more efficiently and responsibly. Those same principles guide how I approach representation."

His professional focus on process, accountability, and practical outcomes informs his view that a congressional representative must understand how federal decisions ripple into daily life at the local level.

## Community involvement

Alex's interest in public service grew from years of community involvement in Philadelphia. He serves on the Philadelphia 9th Ward Republican Committee, where he has developed firsthand experience with how local political structures either enable or obstruct genuine grassroots participation.

> "I believe effective representation requires time spent in the district, clear communication, and an honest understanding of how federal decisions affect daily life."

His committee work exposed him directly to the mechanics of ballot access, signature gathering, and the organizational realities that face any candidate who does not arrive with institutional backing.
